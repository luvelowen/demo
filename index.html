A quiet corner of Seongsu-dong

Mori opened in 2019 in a former textile warehouse a few minutes from Seoul Forest. We kept the brick, the steel windows, and the concrete floor, and built the rest around two long wooden tables — most regulars have a seat they head straight for.

The pour-over list changes when the beans do, not on a fixed schedule. Cups are ceramic, tables are shared, and we don't do to-go cups for anyone dining in.
